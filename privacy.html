Oathway UK Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Oathway UK is an independent study app that helps you prepare for the Life in the UK test. It is not affiliated with the Home Office, GOV.UK or any government body. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to what the app needs.

Information we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your study details. What you enter or create in the app: first name, test date, help language and voice settings, test scores, progress, mistakes, saved questions and checklist ticks.
  • Purchases. Whether you bought the unlock. Payments are handled by the app store; we never see your card details.
  • Problem reports. If you report a problem with a question: the question, the reason you chose and any note you write.
  • Technical information. Like any app, basic technical data (such as your IP address, app version and phone system) is processed to deliver the app and its audio. If a voice recording fails to play, the app sends a short anonymous note so we can fix it. We do not use advertising IDs.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, saving your progress so it follows you to any phone, and playing explanations in your language.
  • To unlock what you bought.
  • To fix questions, translations and recordings that don't work.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.

We use your information to provide the service you asked for, to meet legal obligations (such as keeping purchase records), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your information for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: sign-in and cloud storage, purchase processing and content delivery. They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored with established cloud providers, which may be in the United Kingdom, the United States or other countries. Where information is processed outside the UK, it is protected by safeguards recognised under UK law. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and study data while your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted. Records of purchases may be kept as needed for billing, fraud prevention and legal reasons. Problem reports and recording notes may be kept for up to 12 months to improve the app's content.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your details any time in the app (Profile).
  • Reset your study progress in Profile → Reset all progress.
  • Delete your account and data: see how to delete your account.

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit or stop using it, and ask for your data in a portable format. Email us (below) and we will reply within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Oathway UK is made for adults preparing for the Life in the UK test. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.

Delete your Oathway UK account

You can delete your Oathway UK account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Oathway UK and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in with Google again.

By email

Send an email from the address you sign in with to vinith0140@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Oathway UK account. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Oathway UK sign-in (name, email address and profile picture).
  • Everything saved to your account: name, test date, help language, settings, scores, progress, mistakes, saved questions and checklist.
  • Problem reports you sent.
  • The study data on your phone.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records. These are kept by the app store under its own policies; we may keep a record of what was bought, as needed for billing, refunds and legal reasons.
  • Anonymous recording notes. Short notes about voice recordings that failed to play. They never contained your name, email or account, and are deleted within 12 months.

Good to know

  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
  • Deleting your account does not refund purchases. For refunds, contact the app store.
  • To start over without deleting your account, use Profile → Reset all progress.
